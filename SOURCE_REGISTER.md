# SOURCE_REGISTER: سجل المراجع والمصادر العلمية المعتمدة

1. **معمارية المحولات واللغة الطبيعية (Transformers & Language Modeling)**:
   - Vaswani et al. (2017), *Attention Is All You Need*, Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS).
   - Brown et al. (2020), *Language Models are Few-Shot Learners* (GPT-3), NeurIPS.
   - Touvron et al. (2023), *LLaMA: Open and Efficient Foundation Language Models*, Meta AI.

2. **هندسة الأوامر والاستدلال (Prompting & Reasoning)**:
   - Wei et al. (2022), *Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models*, NeurIPS.
   - Yao et al. (2023), *Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models*, NeurIPS.
   - Kojima et al. (2022), *Large Language Models are Zero-Shot Reasoners*, NeurIPS.

3. **هندسة السياق واسترجاع المعرفة (Context Engineering & RAG)**:
   - Lewis et al. (2020), *Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks*, NeurIPS.
   - Liu et al. (2023), *Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts*, Stanford University / TACL.
   - Gao et al. (2024), *Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models: A Survey*.

4. **معمارية الوكلاء الذكية والأنظمة المستقلة (Agent Architectures & Multi-Agent Systems)**:
   - Yao et al. (2023), *ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models*, ICLR.
   - Shinn et al. (2023), *Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning*, NeurIPS.
   - Park et al. (2023), *Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior*, ACM UIST.
   - Wu et al. (2023), *AutoGen: Enabling Next-Gen LLM Applications via Multi-Agent Conversation Framework*, Microsoft Research.

5. **الحوكمة، الرقابة، والأمان السيادي (AI Governance & Safety)**:
   - NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), National Institute of Standards and Technology (2023).
   - ISO/IEC 42001:2023, *Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system*.
   - European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act, 2024) — High-Risk Systems & Human Oversight mandates.
